About the Conference

NCATPA' 1 2026

The Laboratory of Theoretical Physics and Didactics of the Faculty of Physics, under the auspices of the Houari Boumediene University of Science and Technology (USTHB), is opening up to the scientific community with the organization of the First National Conference on Theoretical Physics and its Applications.


It is hoped that the conference will make a substantial contribution to fundamental research as well as to the application of theoretical knowledge in key economic sectors. The conference aims to provide a forum for consultation and dialogue between the scientific community and its various partners regarding the challenges and advances in these areas.
